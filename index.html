Fulfilling your every need is our highest priority.

RK DESIGNS creates emotive modern interiors that are distinguished by their warmth, originality and careful attention to detail. Founded on the simple belief that good design brings quality to life, our past and current projects include residential and commercial spaces.

A modern hand mixes contemporary & custom-designed pieces with one-of-a-kind accessories & vintage objects, which combine to create compelling atmospheres that are reassuringly warm and layered with visual interest. The approach is client and site specific, ensuring that no two projects look or feel the same.

Meet the Owner

Rahul Sharma

Founder & Lead Designer

With over 15 years of experience in interior design, Rahul founded RK DESIGNS with a vision to transform spaces into works of art. His philosophy centers on the belief that great design is not just about aesthetics — it's about creating environments that enhance the way people live, work, and connect.

Trained at some of the most prestigious design institutions and having worked on projects across India, Rahul brings a unique blend of global sensibility and local craftsmanship to every project. His work has been featured in numerous design publications and he continues to push the boundaries of contemporary interior design.

About Us

At RK DESIGNS, we believe that every space has a story to tell. Founded with a passion for transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences, we are a Bangalore-based interior design studio specializing in residential, commercial, and hospitality design.

Our approach is deeply collaborative — we work closely with each client to understand their vision, lifestyle, and aspirations. From concept to completion, we bring together creativity, craftsmanship, and attention to detail to deliver interiors that are both beautiful and functional.

Whether it's a cozy home, a luxury villa, a bustling office, or a serene hotel, we pour our heart into every project, ensuring that each space reflects the unique personality of those who inhabit it.
