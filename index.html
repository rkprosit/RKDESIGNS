What interior design services do you offer in Kolkata?
We offer home interiors, modular kitchen design, office interiors, false ceiling installation, layout, and complete turnkey interior solutions for residential and commercial spaces across Kolkata.
How much does interior design cost in Kolkata?

Do you provide free consultation for interior design in Kolkata?
Yes, we offer a free consultation to understand your requirements, discuss design ideas, and provide an estimated quote for your interior design project.
How long does an interior design project take in Kolkata?
Project timelines depend on the scope. A typical 2BHK or 3BHK apartment interior takes 4–8 weeks, while larger commercial projects may take longer. We provide a detailed timeline during consultation.

Yes, we provide end-to-end turnkey interior solutions covering design, material selection, procurement, construction, installation, and project management.

Modular kitchen design involves pre-fabricated cabinet modules that are assembled on-site for efficient storage and style. Yes, we design and install custom modular kitchens in Kolkata with premium finishes, durable materials, and smart layouts tailored to your cooking habits.

Yes, we specialize in custom false ceiling designs using gypsum and POP materials. Our team handles complete installation with integrated lighting design to transform the ambiance of any room in Kolkata.

Absolutely. We design and craft custom wardrobes including sliding doors, hinged doors, and walk-in closets that maximize your storage space while complementing your bedroom decor.
